In-Person and Online

In addition to in-person sessions, I work with students remotely via Zoom. I tailored my process for online tutoring through necessity during covid remote schooling, but I now find that this allows me to work with students from a wider geographic region, such as students on foreign exchange or current students who have moved away to college. Other students like the convenience of meeting from home. To enhance my online tutoring, I use a combination of screensharing, writing together in a shared google doc, and a shared online whiteboard. The shared whiteboard allows both the student and I to write, upload documents and worksheets, and save our notes as a pdf to refer back to later.
